# Volunteer reviewer brief

## What this is

This is a small independent review of an open research project about AI
response quality and agent reliability. The review tests whether the local
evidence rubric is understandable and whether an independent reviewer reaches
similar judgments.

It is a calibration exercise, not a request to endorse the paper or the
results.

## Scope

- 10--15 synthetic cases;
- approximately 20--30 minutes;
- a short rubric and a confidence field;
- no coding or API account required;
- no payment or ongoing commitment.

The cases will not include private data, credentials, employer material, or
unpublished certification content. A separate supervised local packet will be
used; the frozen public reports contain metadata only.

## Review steps

### Before the session

1. Read the [study protocol](https://github.com/julie-elkins/llm-eval-validity/blob/feat/complete-reauthorization-paper/research/response-quality/PROTOCOL.md).
2. Read the [independent review protocol](https://github.com/julie-elkins/llm-eval-validity/blob/feat/complete-reauthorization-paper/research/response-quality/INDEPENDENT_REFERENCE_REVIEW.md).
3. Confirm that you can review 10--15 cases in one sitting.
4. Ask questions about the rubric before beginning.

### During the review

1. Open one case at a time.
2. Read the case objective, required evidence, response, and trace events.
3. Record your judgment before seeing the harness decision or another
   reviewer's judgment.
4. Mark each required field as `yes`, `no`, or `unclear`.
5. Add one short reason for any `no` or `unclear` judgment.
6. Record confidence as high, medium, or low.

Do not infer the provider, output condition, or expected harness result. Do not
upload the packet to another AI tool or public service, and do not post
screenshots or case contents publicly.

### After the review

1. Return the completed review form privately.
2. Do not change an answer after seeing another reviewer's judgment.
3. Join an optional debrief after independent reviews are submitted.
4. Identify any rubric wording that was ambiguous or difficult to apply.

## What the reviewer will judge

For each case, the reviewer will mark:

- whether the evidence is sufficient;
- whether the classification is supported;
- whether the required identifier is present;
- whether the bounded workflow state is supported;
- confidence: high, medium, or low;
- a reason when the rubric and the reviewer's judgment disagree.

An `unclear` answer is allowed. The goal is to expose ambiguity rather than
force agreement.

## What happens after review

Reviewer judgments will be compared with the local evidence-contract result.
The analysis will report agreement, disagreement, unclear judgments, and
disagreement reasons. If two reviewers participate, their agreement will be
reported separately.

Names will not be published without permission. The review will not change the
six frozen provider conditions; any rubric change becomes a new version and a
new study phase.

## Participation

To volunteer, reply with:

1. your relevant experience;
2. whether you can review 10--15 cases in one session;
3. whether you are comfortable with your feedback being quoted anonymously.

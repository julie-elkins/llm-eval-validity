# Task-form release policy

## Public before the pilot

The public repository may expose:

- task identifiers;
- task families;
- target skills;
- learning objectives;
- allowed evidence categories;
- assistance boundaries;
- rubric dimensions;
- public reason codes.

The public repository must not expose the participant-facing wording,
transfer-form wording, scoring key, or reviewer packet before the pilot.

## Private study packet

The private study packet contains:

- baseline forms;
- assisted forms;
- transfer forms;
- participant instructions;
- evaluator packets;
- scoring key;
- calibration cases;
- version and digest metadata.

The packet must be versioned and hashed. A form change after recruitment begins
creates a new protocol version.

## After the pilot

The study may release sanitized task forms after:

1. participant data is de-identified;
2. the answer key is reviewed for accidental private content;
3. the release will not invalidate a planned replication;
4. the paper and venue permit release;
5. the release is recorded as a new artifact version.

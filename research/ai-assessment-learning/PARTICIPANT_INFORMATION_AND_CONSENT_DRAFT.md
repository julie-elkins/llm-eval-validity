# Future participant information and consent — draft

**Status: Future-phase draft only. Not approved for recruitment.**

This document is a study-material draft, not legal advice or an ethics
determination. Recruitment must wait until the applicable review process
confirms whether the activity requires institutional review, an exemption, or
another documented determination.

## Study purpose

This study examines whether an AI-assisted assessment can distinguish a
correct-looking response from evidence that a person can apply the target skill
without AI assistance.

## What participation involves

You may be asked to:

1. complete a short task without AI assistance;
2. complete a matched task with a bounded AI assistant;
3. provide a confidence or verification response;
4. complete a matched transfer task without AI assistance; and
5. answer a short experience questionnaire.

The study is expected to take approximately [duration to be finalized] minutes.

## AI-use boundary

The study will state exactly when AI assistance is allowed. The assistant will
not submit answers for you, access private accounts, or make decisions about
employment, certification, grades, or eligibility.

## Risks

The anticipated risk is low, but participation may involve:

- mild frustration from difficult tasks;
- discomfort from seeing an incorrect or uncertain AI response;
- privacy risk if you enter information that was not requested.

Do not enter confidential, employer-owned, customer, health, financial, or
certification information.

## Benefits

You may not receive a direct personal benefit. The study may help researchers
design AI-assisted assessment systems that distinguish supported learning
evidence from fluent but unsupported performance.

## Data collected

The study may collect:

- randomized participant code;
- task and form identifiers;
- submitted responses needed for scoring;
- confidence or verification fields;
- completion status;
- bounded timing and interaction counts;
- rubric scores;
- transfer-task results.

The public repository will contain aggregate results and metadata-only reason
codes. It will not contain names, contact information, raw responses, answer
keys, or reviewer identities.

## Voluntary participation and withdrawal

Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time without penalty. The
withdrawal process and the date after which deletion is no longer feasible must
be stated before recruitment begins.
